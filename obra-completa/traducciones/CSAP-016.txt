It'll be
It'll be a forbidden love, simply.
It'll be the same damned apple.
It'll be not getting what blurs me
or not being able to do what one feels.

It'll be that I don't know if one must be brave.
Will it be passion crossing the membrane
of doing whatever we please?
It'll be forgetting, it'll be, probably.

Smiles without fictional tears,
memories of those captive glances,
the great story without any action.

It'll be you and your passive will.
It'll be me for my eagerness to be a rogue.
It'll be everything, a world upside down.
