Hearing
I hate you for hugging me with your strong
innocently wicked spell.
It's your colours that make me angry
but it's your soul I want to see you for.

I hate you for catching me —bad luck?—
in your arms... but I'm delighted,
I blame you but I blame my state more
and I hate you but I hate loving you more.

It's love like fire, that much is clear,
a clearing that burns me raw.
A chicken as an engine? Everything is strange.

How hypocritical is it that today I write to you:
yesterday's locomotive is a lighthouse
that a soul that blinds is a fugitive soul?

(deswaze)

Ví. Ab. Al.
