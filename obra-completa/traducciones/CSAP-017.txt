Not acting made me a fool.
The collision of two trains.
The trench battle
where you simply wait
for them not to take hostages.

Seeing them fighting
a war of attrition
where disaster could be felt
and it was slipping away...

Not acting made me a fool,
for: they had to crash.
At last someone's going to win!
Win yes, but at what price.

Surprise! The red rain
will come from the crossfire
(apparently ignored).
You'll see what misfortune it throws...

RIGHT UNDER MY DAMNED NOSE
THEY CRASHED, THEY KNOCKED DOWN MY WORLD.
AND IN LESS THAN 10 SECONDS
THEY WRECKED MY LIFE

Ví. Ab. Al.

(baymi)
