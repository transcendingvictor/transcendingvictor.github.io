Terrorist
If I understood that voice that murmurs.
If I knew what it is telling me
that broken voice. I don't understand it!
So faint, so short, so unsure.

If I listened to that voice that murmurs
in the distance. The voice I don't intend
to understand and I hope it's leaving
with all its questions, its madness.

You who pass by and live at the wrong hours.
Throw off the mask. Look at the hill.
I am reality (your greatest horror).

Shut up! —I told it—. That's why now
I keep on gulping down petrol
to feed my inner flame.

Ví. Ab. Al.
