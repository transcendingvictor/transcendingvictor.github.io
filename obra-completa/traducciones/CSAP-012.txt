If there were...
Life shuts itself off
invaded by death.
You're no longer the one in charge,
almost nobody can see you.

You walk a tightrope
bounded by nothing.
You don't do what you feel like:
A PATH ALREADY MARKED
Behind the perverted flowers
you are the stagnant water.
The memories you don't forget
leave your candle out.

Too late now for firsts
since you've swallowed the bait,
walking costs you more now,
you're no longer a little stream.

If you went back to the past
to a less narrow place
How much would you have changed?
(If you regret what you've done)

What's interesting is life
and you hope it doesn't vanish
in your corroded grave
when the body wastes away.

(muerte)

Ví. Ab. Al.
