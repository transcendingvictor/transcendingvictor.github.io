Fear?
Even if at first you don't like the taste
since in the new air you cough
(when everything is strange) don't handcuff yourself,
try to develop your splendour.

Because when we start the engine
and everything tastes like the juice of gods
we are who we are, there are no poses
there are no fears, there is no pain.

But the waterfall comes and pinches.
The dream ends in an eternal shock.
You don't know what to do in an earthquake.

Suffering leaves you a pinch
of something you own in hell:
an empty glass on a broken boat.

(hiacia)

Ví. Ab. Al.
