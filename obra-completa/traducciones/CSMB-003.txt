Routine alienates joy
What would happen if today were your last day,
if your vital melody went out now
What would happen if your time ran out,
if you were going to die, what would happen?

What would happen if this were the last time
you recognised yourself in a mirror.
What would happen when you looked again.
What would happen to your maturity.

What would happen if you forgot who you love
If you never again remembered who they are;
if all that mass of dense pleasure
broke down into nothing.

Do you think you wouldn't care
never sitting in the armchair again,
never hearing that song again,
never getting naked in the sea again.

Never stroking their hair again,
never doing great feats again,
never being nature again,
never admiring the sky again.

What would happen if nothing mattered a jot.
What would happen if we couldn't think
about what might or might not happen.
What would happen? That we wouldn't be afraid

Ví. Ab. Al.
