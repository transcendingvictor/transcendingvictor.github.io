Fleetingness
If someone ruins YOUR happiness
or someone awful takes off your blindfold,
don't stop walking, follow YOUR path.
Always dare to understand your truth.

If you've made up a reality
and suddenly your tent breaks
rebuild, repair, make amends,
you don't want, outside, to see such evil

Why are feelings so fragile?
Hope catches the same carriage.
Why did I have breath before and now not?

Why does one word crack
my heart much more than a slow love?
Unreal joy, an illusion.

Ví. Ab. Al.
