Fleeting as your lips spitting at me.
Fleeting as the message in the bottle.
Fleeting as the winter that stays.
Fleeting as the fire in the grove.
Fleeting and hot as a star.

So fleeting, so fleeting and so beautiful
the endless path, the trail
(the truth?) that lashes me without my being able
to understand anything. To understand her...

The shadows guide me as I walk.
The night urges me if, in my ear,
it tells me I have to kill her.

Fool. I kill her. I've lit the spark
and I haven't learnt to understand, to love.
The world so clear... Me so lost...

Ví. Ab. Al.
