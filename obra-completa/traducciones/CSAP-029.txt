My ears hurt when I hear you
[insert verse for enjambment]
If a thousand passions make you ramble
my thousand pressures may confuse you.

My ears hurt when I hear you
go quiet —a noise impossible to affirm—.
And it fell and I couldn't listen to you...
...and went quiet... and I could no longer avoid you.

Because I bite the lifelines,
because "fire with fire", and I mess up
your lying orders like a dimwit.

Because the sun shines and its breast doesn't hurt.
Because the sane always get given more rope
and you shine differently...       but oh well.
