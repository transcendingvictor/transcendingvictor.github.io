Don't send me any more messages from Valencia
Don't send me any more messages
telling me about your great holidays.
Don't talk to me about your parties.
Don't tell me about your crazy stuff.
Don't send me any more messages.

Don't send me any more messages
about your unforgettable camping trips,
about your starry nights,
about your long walks.
Don't send me any more messages.

Don't send me any more messages
saying you miss me a lot
from that faraway cloud,
the cloud where I keep you.
Don't send me any more messages.

Don't send me any more messages,
or photos that remind me who you are.
Don't ever call me either.
Don't ever kill me either.
Don't send me any more messages.

Don't send me any more messages
that I might not bear to hear.
Or send them to me if you want
if in this lapse it doesn't matter
that you send me more messages.

Ví. Ab. Al.

(lialytea)
