doesn't just die
Reality doesn't die   it just transforms
like the soft plasticine of your legs.

My little eyes look for you   just to look for you   to have something to do   but they look for you.
Your gaze frustrates me   just to frustrate me   —it has nothing to do with it—   but it frustrates me
I can't find it   it kills me
it is lost   it doesn't kill me
it can't be found   it transforms me.

And meanwhile,
reality clenches its teeth
to go on existing.

Reality doesn't die   it just grows fat
like a willow that wraps you and doesn't weep.

Your legs melt in the opposite direction
because every day you are less tall.
Sinking into the quicksand knees.
Every day you lose yourself,
you strike yourself down looking at yourself, you-y-you tremble.

And meanwhile,
reality chews its own molars,
clenches its fists
to go on existing

Reality doesn't die   it is just born
as if it were Rapunzel's blond hair..

...a sea scent makes me fall in love.
When you smile for pretend I smile for real.
When you laugh for pretend I laugh,
really I laugh when someone jokes and I believe it.
Even if you're not here, if I see you...
...if the animal rejection that runs through every retch
weren't the dislocation of the jaw/You cut me off.
You undress. "Fuck your feelings" —you shout. You whisper: "Fuck your feelings"

And meanwhile,
reality clenches its teeth and its fists
holds its breath as best it can
tears its own guts
swallows
shrinks
keeps its gaze rigid
holds its pulse steady
—more than its heart—
bites its lip
with a solemn face
pretends to be asleep
not dead, dozing off,
not dead, tired,
swallows,
this time, swallowing its words too
cries
to go on existing.

Reality doesn't die   it just moults.

Ví. Ab. Al.
