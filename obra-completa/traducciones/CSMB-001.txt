WE MUST
We must try to imagine getting out of this current,
we must dig where the one who lies creates unhappiness,
we must show off, and win, and sing while we can,
we must shout to feel and to make everything different,
we must ignore so we can think and not get into a mess,
we must search where everyone looks but with glasses of disbelief,
we must hit, we must break, we must savour disasters
to calm down and go mad and watch how it moves ahead,
we must sound because otherwise, because otherwise everything is gross,
we must avoid the boring day that twists and isn't heard,
we must abolish passivity, we must call...   and call...  and call...
because in the end inspiration moves out,
doubts don't matter,
damned conjectures! That get lost
for mattering
to no one
since nothing is going to change.

Ví. Ab. Al.
