One-way
A loss that wasn't yours.
Needless pain that you suffered.
A false feeling you didn't see
for someone who didn't feel the same.

She seemed a pleasant person
but you got obsessed and you are sad,
it doesn't matter who you were or who you used to be
if on the other side nobody saw you.

What for? Getting attached for no reason
Why?   To the wrong person
who in no case would have loved you.

Now you'll never give too much
importance (because of Cupid)
to the one who looks at you as if it were nothing.
