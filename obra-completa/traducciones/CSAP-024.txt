Vaguely smiling
Lying down in the field,
lying down in the dark.
Before a starry sky,
before a sky of doubts.

Fireflies flying
in the black dome.
Little by little trying
to make out the Perseids

I wonder, curious,
thinking —maybe— of harms,
whether we might be
like shooting stars.

Travellers and restless
they never stop running,
the stars you look at
and never see again.

They get lost in the night
with Cancer and with Pisces,
they ask you not to cry,
I won't lose you, Lily.

The things I think sometimes!
This sky invades me
with ideas that come
like shooting stars.

Ví. Ab. Al.

(lialytea)
