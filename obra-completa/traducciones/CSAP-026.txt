Living is easy with your eyes closed
It's easy to be, with your eyes closed,
one more person in this life
who because of their outside situation forgets
all their dreams, always unfinished.

Living is easy too if switched off
are the lamps that show the way out,
and you're with one hand on the drink
and in the other a book, stories never begun.

Opening them isn't enough, for you see
nothing if there's nothing to light you up:
in the night there are no different things.

The match won't strike, you no longer breathe
oxygen, you ask for it to end:
the whisky soaks the following pages.

Ví. Ab. Al.
