Ignorance + Freedom
Waiting in silence is always hard
when your hurricane soul screeches,
for wanting to sail and being empty,
in this short, dark little stream.

It's frustrating to begin to make out the wall
at the end of the fleeting crossing
without having gone with you, Sofía,
to that corner where there is no future.

This river no longer flows so slowly
and my silence angers this soul of mine,
she grows while I stay put.

How am I going to seize the moment?
If moments without you are nothing.
—Carpe diem! —they tell me, and I can't.

Ví. Ab. Al.

(ríos franceses sabiondos)
