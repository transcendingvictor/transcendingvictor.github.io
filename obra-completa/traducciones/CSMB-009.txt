Repatriating rage
There is a congress with compressed prisoners, unharmed and oppressed like dogs.
But sorry for perpetrating matricides on parts of a perverse building with more vices than watching the verbiage of royal verbs:
lying, killing, mounting, mutating.
Consenting to evil...
With feeling, loving, with counting on you...

There is a congress with verses that have managed
to keep guiding mistreated truths.
They have tried to lie and have mis-lied
nursing imaginary images

There is a congress where the only thing admitted
is the industrialisation of consciences,
the perfect science of obedience
that wraps you up but doesn't interest you.

There is a congress where the deputies
Argue about changing their prefix:
swapping the "di" for "im" or just "puta"...
They will decide, I never choose.

There is a congress where they represent
imprisoned, repressed premonitions.
There is a congress where the only ones who sit
are saints voted in by our moans.

There is a congress where freedom
has been subtly confused
among different equal colours...
There is no freedom between your money and your life!
(There will never be freedom if passion
Or any mantra that lies to your mind
Transforms memory or perverts it)

There is a congress where you're forced to believe in the notion of a nation lacking truth and,
long live the evident oblivion! To force a consented nonsense of the death of reason.
If the explanation is the passion you feel for an idealised image that argues that people, despite being different, must be equal before the fatherland...

Voting is avoiding freedom.
Voting is vetoing your actions
From their national sovereignty.

Lying is tossing myths in a blanket
That kill the truth,
They deny the possibility
Of hearing my dry screams.

There is a congress where you're forced to believe in the joke of a blessed nation that exists because it exists by its own notion that whoever was born in this portion of war between idiots who kill for fun are compatriots of the tambourine country.

Because there is no greater pleasure than watching the unfriend run and shooting until the heart stops.
Because there is no greater urge than imposing on the unborn the duty of protecting the chosen head of the state that has taken them in and that has taken from them the power to choose to live far from a democracy, which is a demos from Greece, which is where I write.
Because there is no greater love than love for love, love in itself
NO! Not love for oneself
NO!  Not the love for a nation or a state that lasts through the dark flag waiting for meaning.
