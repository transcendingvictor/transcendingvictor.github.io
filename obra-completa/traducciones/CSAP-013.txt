Stupid brave ones.
It pulls me out of my calm routine.
My flat path turns and moves.
On the serene scale it rains
and the water leaves it unsettled.

Cold, heat, a stalled passion.
Any plane that takes me to the clouds.
I look for a flower that feels me and feeds me,
and shines on its own, unlimited.

I show up with my hungry drive.
I tear petals and take life.
My tears explode, I die slowly.

How someone tends to run away,
lets themselves be invaded by a feeling
and for their lack of calm kills themselves!

(hiacia)

Ví. Ab. Al.
