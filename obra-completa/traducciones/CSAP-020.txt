Sincerity.
People's bodies are empty
when I look
all of a sudden
and their sigh is heard, suffocated

The censored howl, captive,
with no truth.
A beggar
who knows all that's left is to wait.

And how those damned dogs wait,
clinging
to the event
that will quickly leave them stripped bare.

I will kill amateur matter
merely comic,
it is a duty
to punish the hypocritical shells.

Ví. Ab. Al.
