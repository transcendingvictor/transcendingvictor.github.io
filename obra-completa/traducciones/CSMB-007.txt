The truths howl.
I masturbate thinking of rockets, that don't fly.
It disturbs me to hear the truth.
It makes me fall in love to watch slaughtered flocks
like white sponges bathed in their own blood,
but wolves terrify me... Today there are so many,
so many dressed in white, so many liars.

I am a huntress,
a winter huntress,        white and hot.
I embrace truths with bazooka blasts and I hunt,
I hunt rockets, rockets? No, heads.
