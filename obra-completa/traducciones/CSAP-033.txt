Benalmádena
You have inflated my body  subtly and slowly
and gently tied  my waist to life.
Without meaning to you pulled  and I am obedient
and I have taken as true  your singing, your beauty.

Your presence murmurs  smiling words,
and the air, your dancing  fully inhabits it,
and you avoid soaking  people with dramas
showing the sweetness  your voice stirs in us.

You have inflated my chest:  it doesn't burst, it isn't full
and sometimes I say  it's something passing,
but flying with you  is sailing without brakes.
Then I go and understand  that I do love you a little.

Ví. Ab. Al.
