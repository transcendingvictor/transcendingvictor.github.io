infiniteLIFE DEADLY
I don't want you to get upset:
—You're someone who gets moved—.
Only realities exist.
There is no absolute truth.

Though there ought to be one,
a determined perfection.
Your teeth now are pearls
and your hair a waterfall.

This is made for a reason
fruit of idealisation
for: "You're outside, I'm coming out,
my heart is pounding!"

We need Romeos
who turn up and give it all.
There'll have to be ugly ones too,
love and all that nonsense.

What we are looking for
has to be everyday
something we all can be
that is good, sane and healthy.

And it has to be true
the poetic won't do
if it's a calamity,
at least, let it be ethical.

It can't be anything else
"GOOD" and for everyone
so that even the wife
of your best tramp understands.

Incomprehensible it is now
if open, never closed
a Pandora's box
is a truncated door.

Why is crying no use?
When it would have been so easy.
One drop and off to dream,
DREAM, because it never existed.

So don't you get upset
because that is not the truth.
—The truth is there are no truths—.
Degrees of neutrality.

They'll be decisions, Sofía.
You let go, you come and go.
Will you open the box one day?
Will you smile a while longer?

But since this is poetry
and I don't want to end badly:
Would you seriously enjoy
a river with no end?

Don't think about your answer,
of course it will be wrong
there are never right answers
nobody dived through the sea.

(felicidad)

Ví. Ab. Al.
