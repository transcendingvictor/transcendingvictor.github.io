Money will never go out of fashion
however many moralists preach
that happiness lies in the nuances
and that gold leaves people alone.

Money will never go out of fashion
since it gives us what we ask of it,
any dirty good is money, look,
we already crawl for anything.

There's a surplus of things we don't need
and the essential is what we don't understand,
are we all like this, the inhuman ones?

We are horrible, me at least
if I happen to find twenty quid
I won't care if it's right, I'll keep it.
