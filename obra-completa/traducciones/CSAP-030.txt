Illness: boredom.
Don't think it's out of anger or laziness
if you don't hear me talk about boredom.
I can't manage to get bored, though I try,
for fear of lightly brushing sadness.

The reason I don't master such a skill:
the constant swaying of a thought
that causes the distracting suffering.
Heart, get out of my head.

Ví. Ab. Al.
(deswaze)
