CHANGE ME
My world like a torn-off tree
because of you, because of your words, Sara,
because of your disgust, your rejection, your murderous
face. I'm weakened now...

You are impenetrable, proven.
A wall whose curious rod
will push me... If I dug my grave...
It will end in a poem, proven.

If you're not so bad, why do you attack me?
If you're not so submissive, why do you obey,
slave, the patterns you criticise?

That personality you pull out
of your childish world. Cheap ideas
with a touch of ignorance, are tasty.

(sara)

Ví. Ab. Al.
