TODAY
One more day that stays in memory.
One more day that fades away.
One more day that, when demanding vitality,
is crushed with indifference.

One more day and my features show
a passive neutrality similar to yesterday's,
and I say similar because today I remembered
that I haven't done anything again, anything exciting.

One more day that makes no sense on its own.
Another day that is a simple transition
to a perfect, idealised future I don't understand
and I have huge doubts about wanting to go on, like this.

Another of many days and weeks without something
that makes me dance over the darkness that invades me
that makes me breathe deep, that makes me sob warm...
that makes me forget it's one more day.

Another day that bores me and tires me
that sinks me into this pit that gets deeper.
Another day that drains me and takes away my will to swim.
That I don't know anymore if I care about drowning.

One more day, one more day, one more day, one more day.
One more day that is now my present and won't matter.
Another one where you can't appreciate how the sun's rays fade.
One more day, that seems like a day too many and is really a day less.

Ví. Ab. Al.
