With your four elements I get confused.
Spreading your liberal perfume
you took me to the heavens, like the wind.
Like water you re-refreshed my breath
and soaked me with your cultured humour.

You lit the animal in my body
that you consumed like fire, slowly.
Like the earth you gave me sustenance
guiding me to your springtime peace.

You managed to get into the deep
without quite fitting, to be honest.
With your four elements I get confused.

Because air, water, fire and earth I want,
but you are a hyperbole of the world:
volcano, tornado, earthquake.
Downpour
