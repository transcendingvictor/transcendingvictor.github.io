## Historia

I wrote it around 12 May 2018 in a notebook, in Vicálvaro (the end of my fourth year of secondary school, *4.º de la ESO*), just after a Friday and before my trip to Lyon, and I published it on 28 June. It is very much of that time: my search for feeling at ease, for being well.

## Comentario de la forma

It is a classical sonnet in hendecasyllables (ABBA ABBA CDE DCE), and the closed form already shuts the speaker in, like the people around him. The **anaphora** of «Solo» opens the first three lines («Solo estoy…», «Solo inmerso…», «Solo por no escapar…»): the word comes back like a blow and, written without an accent, each time it can mean either "alone" or "only". In line 5 the same words return in another order, «Estoy rodeado solo de gente», and *solo* changes place and meaning. Even the rhyme is closed in: *gente* ("people") rhymes with itself (lines 1 and 5), and *gente* degrades into «¡Gentuza…!» ("Riff-raff…!", line 7). The second quatrain closes the circle with «Solo y rodeado completamente» ("Alone and completely surrounded").

The **anadiplosis** (*completamente* ending line 8 and opening line 9) pushes from the quatrains into the tercets without a breath. There the rhythm breaks with **enjambments**: «gente / ruin» had announced it, but in the tercets they even jump from one stanza to the next: «Pasar de quien pasa / de pasar» ("To not care about those who don't care / about not caring"), and «Pasear conocimiento / falso» ("To parade knowledge / that is false"), where the adjective falls at the start of the line and undoes what had just been said. The **wordplay** *pasar, pasa, pasar, pasear* makes you dizzy, like the very idea of leaving and not leaving, and *conocimiento* rhymes with *argumento* from the previous tercet, as if turning back. The ending closes with **parallelism** and **antithesis**: «y te ahoga el vacío, y te ahoga la masa» ("and the void drowns you, and the crowd drowns you"). And the "me" of the whole poem becomes "you": the speaker looks at himself from outside.

## Explicación

The poem is a drawing: a person in the centre, people all around and, in between, a void. The quatrains paint it by circling the same image. «Solo estoy rodeado de gente»: I am alone, or there is nothing but people. The world ignores him, and he doesn't escape, without it being clear whether that is because of him or because of them. The coldness that devours him seems to come from outside, from those around him, but it is felt inside. Then the spear is thrown back: he doesn't understand that crying «gentuza» ("riff-raff") either. The centre and the outside are at odds, and the eighth line confirms it: "Alone and completely surrounded".

In the tercets the protagonist gets tired. It is not the tiredness of switching off, but of "I'm tired, I have to act". That war of reproaches is a film with no plot, and the way out he considers is to leave, to not care about those who don't care about not caring: people who say they don't care but in fact do, because they meddle, surround and condition. He has to take his knowledge out of there, parade it somewhere else.

But the enjambment breaks that way out: the knowledge is "false". Whose? Theirs, his, or what the poem itself is saying about them and about him. The image, though very clear, ripples: it seems about to get out and then goes back in. And in the end it traps him. The void the crowd leaves, the gap others make around you, is both a physical void and an inner one, because people feed on their social relationships. The void drowns you and the crowd drowns you: the same thing seen from inside and from outside.

## Anotaciones por verso

- "Solo" [sentido]: Without an accent (as the Spanish Academy has recommended since 2010), *solo* means both "alone" and "only". The poem doesn't choose.
- "la frialdad asesina omnipresente" [sentido]: "The murderous, omnipresent coldness": does it come from them or from inside?
- "Gentuza que no entiendo por qué llora" [sentido]: The spear is thrown back: the coldness also goes outwards.
- "cansado" [sentido]: "Tired", not as in switching off and going to sleep, but as in "I'm tired, I have to act".
- "las películas sin argumento" [sentido]: "Films with no plot": the war of reproaches, the circle that leads nowhere.
- v11-12 [sentido]: They say they don't care (*pasar*), but they do: they meddle. They "don't care about not caring". So it's time to not care about them.
- "falso" [sentido]: The enjambment breaks the way out. Is it their knowledge that is false, mine, or this poem's?
- v14 [sentido]: Void and crowd are the same thing, seen from inside and from outside.
- "(algalex)": Hidden dedication: it is for two people.

## Forma

- v1 [forma]: 11 syllables, with no elision between «Solo | estoy».
- v4 [forma]: «frialdad», with «fria» as one syllable.
- v6 [forma]: «ruin» as two syllables (ru-ín), as in the tradition.
- v12 [forma]: «pasear» as three syllables.
